# v8.3.3 — GL Scan Engine

## Why v8.3.2 was rejected

The workbook showed a real source-to-output failure:

- PJ!I9 (KNOXPORT net purchase) = **915.4017857**
- CDJ!S17 = **Miscellaneous**
- CDJ!T17 = **1025.25**
- visible GL!Miscellaneous / JAN was blank
- GL_V8_CALC!Miscellaneous / JAN was **2980.4017857**

The prior RefreshGL path therefore could produce calculation data without reliably materializing the visible GL.

## v8.3.3 execution model

RefreshGL, RefreshGLIntoSheet, and RefreshAllGL now delegate to ScanGL.

The scanner:

1. scans CDJ rows and carries the journal month marker;
2. ignores monthly TOTAL rows;
3. resolves fixed CDJ account columns;
4. resolves the CDJ Sundry Account text as the actual COA;
5. derives the Sundry posting from the row's balancing legs instead of trusting a stale T/U amount;
6. scans CRJ;
7. scans GJ with the documented COA mappings;
8. aggregates by normalized COA + month;
9. writes the visible GL G/H and cumulative I;
10. rebuilds GL_V8_CALC from the same scan;
11. appends a v8.3.3 scan audit row.

- no compile error;

- visible GL!Miscellaneous January reflects the scanned amount;

Do not merge until that runtime evidence exists.
