# v8.2 GL Refresh Review — can `RefreshGL` refresh all COAs every month?

**Verdict: NO — not as shipped.**

`Global-Smile_2026-v8.2-final-review.xlsm` does **not** contain the reviewed v8.2
`modGL*` engine. Its `RefreshGL` is the legacy `modEngine` implementation, and on
this workbook it **aborts before writing anything**:

> `RefreshGL error 1004: We can't do that to a merged cell.`

Even with that fixed, the legacy engine writes a **552-row GL that is out of
balance by 1,139.00**, leaves **30 of 46 accounts at zero for all 12 months**,
**9 of 12 months at zero**, and destroys the reviewed 13-row block layout.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — BLOCKER: `RefreshGL` cannot execute on this workbook

`gl.Range("B11:I11").value = Array("Date","Day","Reference","Explanation","Account
Title","Debit","Credit","Ending Balance")` targets a merged band → error 1004,
`MsgBox "RefreshGL error 1004..."`, `Exit Sub`. Nothing is refreshed, silently
leaving the previous (unbalanced) GL in place.

### F2 — BLOCKER: no reconciliation gate; the GL is written out of balance

`RefreshGL` never calls `ValidateGLConsistency` and has no `|Debit − Credit|` test
before writing. Measured result: **debit 255,006.75 vs credit 256,145.75 →
difference −1,139.00**, written to the GL and announced as a success.

### F3 — Coverage: "all COAs × 12 months" is only structural

552 rows are written, but only **35 of 552** account-month cells carry a value:
**30 of 46 accounts are zero for the entire year**, and **months APR–DEC are zero
in every account**. Accounts with any activity: Cash in Bank, Petty Cash Fund,
Advances to Employees, Input VAT, Leasehold Improvements, Dental Equipment, VAT
Payable, Sales, Clinic Material and Supplies, both `Dep'n Expense` accounts,
Repair and Maintenance, Pantry Supplies, Office Supplies, Miscellaneous,
Transportation and Travel (source activity exists for JAN/FEB/MAR only).

### F4 — CDJ month detection silently drops rows (2,525.25 lost)

`GLScanCDJ` reads the month from col C per row and **does not carry it forward**
(unlike `GLScanGJ`, which does). CDJ labels the month only on the first row of each
monthly block (`C15=C16=JAN`, `C60=FEB`, `C70=MAR`, …). Rows 17 and 18 of the JAN
block have no label and are dropped: `F=-1025.25 / S=Miscellaneous / T=1025.25` and
`F=-1500 / N=1500 / T=1500`. **2,525.25 of debits and credits never reach the GL**,
and any future multi-row month block would lose all but its first row.

### F5 — GJ label `Cost of Revenue` is unmapped (1,139.00 lost)

`GLAcct()` has no case for it; the engine logs `GJ | Cost of Revenue | 1139` into
`GL_AUDIT` and drops the debit, which is the entire −1,139.00 imbalance.
The v8.2 repo mapping (`COST OF REVENUE → Clinic Material and Supplies`) is *not*
what the workbook implements.


### F6 — The refreshed GL destroys the reviewed block layout

The writer is contiguous (`r = 13`, `r = r + 1` for 552 rows) while the reviewed
mapping — and `modGLGate.ValidateV8Structure` — expects `13 + 13*b`. After one run
the titles sit at rows 13…564 with step **1**, rows 565–609 (the old Insurance
block area) are cleared, and the gate would return `False` at block 43
(`13+13*43 = 572` is now empty).

### F7 — GL titles are re-labelled from a hardcoded array

`gl.Cells(r,6).value = a(i)` overwrites the sheet's account names with
`GLAccounts()`. Row 205 is rewritten as `Retained Earnings (Deficit)`, i.e. the
duplicate `Paid-Up Capital` block is silently renamed. Any divergence between the
sheet's COA and the array is masked instead of reported.

### F8 — `GL_AUDIT` history is destroyed and the layout changes

`aw.Cells.Clear` + `A1:C1 = Source | Unmapped Detail | Amount` wipes the previous
`Timestamp | Status | Summary | Detail` rows (v8.0 baseline, v8.1 mapped, v8.2
static precheck) and drops the status/verdict column — the unmapped 1,139.00 is
recorded with no PASS/HOLD verdict.

### F9 — `GL_V8_CALC` is never refreshed (stale, contradicts the GL)

`RefreshGL` writes only `GL` and `GL_AUDIT`. After the run `GL_V8_CALC!D2` still
shows `7,025.25` for Cash in Bank month 1 while the new `GL!H13` shows `4,500`.

### F10 — CRJ months come from block position, and appended rows are ignored

`GLScanCRJ` locates `TOTAL` rows and assigns months by **ordinal position**, using
the col-D label only as a "this is a block end" flag. Here position and label agree
(12 blocks JAN…DEC, TOTAL rows 25/36/44/53/61/69/78/87/95/103/111/118), so nothing
is mis-booked today — but it is a latent shift risk, and the stray balances at CRJ
rows 581–582 (`26,480 / 2,837.14 / 23,642.86`, outside every block) are **never
scanned**: data appended after the December block is lost silently. (Observation:
the JAN block holds receipts whose Entry-Log timestamps are 8/2/2026, 8/31/2026 and
9/10/2026 — worth confirming against the intended accounting period, since the
engine never checks transaction dates.)

### F11 — Monthly cadence is not supported by this design

`RefreshGL` rebuilds the whole year from the journals each time (`bal = 0`, then
months 1→12). There is no opening-balance/period carry-forward, and
`ClearContents` over `B12:I1000` wipes anything below the written rows. A monthly
run is only safe while the complete year-to-date journals remain in the CDJ / CRJ /
GJ blocks; clearing a closed month's journal would turn that month's GL back into
zeros. `RefreshAllGL(targetSheet)` in the workbook also ignores its year argument —
calling `RefreshAllGL(2026)` runs an auto-detected year.

### F12 — The shipped numbers do not match the v8.2 review numbers (fully explained)

| | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| v8.2 static precheck (`V8.2_FINAL_REVIEW`, `v8.2_MANIFEST.json`) | 258,671.00 | 258,671.00 |
| less CDJ rows 17–18 skipped (F4) | −2,525.25 | −2,525.25 |
| less GJ `Cost of Revenue` unmapped (F5) | −1,139.00 | — |
| **measured engine output (probes A and C)** | **255,006.75** | **256,145.75** |

The v8.2 BAS modules were never imported into this workbook (the manifest already
says "NOT CERTIFIED"), and the mapping tables differ (`Supplies` → `Office Supplies`
in the workbook vs `Clinic Material and Supplies` in the repo).

### Data-hygiene notes

- GL header: `I11 = "Credit"` although column I holds the ending balance
  (`RefreshGL` corrects this to "Ending Balance" — a by-product of its header write).
- Year cells disagree: `CDJ!C14 = 2025`, `CRJ!D9 = 2025`, `GJ!B10 = 2026`,
  `GL!B12 = 2026`.
- The shipped GL already contains 7 stray non-zero cells (Rent, Clinician's Fee-\*)
  totalling `907,749.86` debit vs `7,025.25` credit — the GL sheet has never been
  produced by a clean, gated refresh.

## 4. Answer to "can it refresh all COAs every month?"

| Requirement | As shipped | After fixing only the merge (F1) |
|---|---|---|
| Runs at all | **NO** — 1004 abort | yes, ~1 s |
| Writes a row for every COA × every month | not reached | yes — 552 rows (46 × 12) |
| Every COA carries real monthly figures | **NO** | **NO** — 30 of 46 accounts zero all year, 9 of 12 months zero |
| GL stays balanced | **NO** | **NO** — −1,139.00 |
| Unmapped source labels resolved | **NO** — 1,139.00 (`Cost of Revenue`) | **NO** |
| CDJ/CRJ rows fully captured | **NO** — 2,525.25 dropped (CDJ rows 17–18) | **NO** |
| Reviewed GL block layout preserved | n/a | **NO** — stride 13 → stride 1 |
| `GL_V8_CALC` / audit consistent with GL | **NO** | **NO** |
| Safe to repeat monthly | **NO** | only with the complete year-to-date journals kept in place |

**Conclusion:** `RefreshGL` in this workbook is a full-year, gated-free rebuild that
currently cannot execute, and even when it can, it is a *rewrite of 46
account rows × 12 month rows* — not a correct monthly COA refresh. The claim
"refreshes all COAs every month" is true only in the row-count sense.

## 5. Required fixes (in order)

1. **Unblock** — remove the `B11:C11 / D11:I12` merges or write the 8 header cells
   individually before the array write (F1).
2. **Fail closed** — abort the refresh unless `|Debit − Credit| ≤ 0.01`,
   `Unmapped = 0` and `Invalid = 0`, reusing the reviewed `modGLGate` /
   `ValidateGLConsistency` behaviour (F2).
3. **CDJ month carry-forward** — keep the last non-zero month like `GLScanGJ`, and
   report every row whose month cannot be determined instead of skipping it (F4).
4. **Map or reject** `Cost of Revenue` (and any other label without a COA home);
   record the decision in `COA_MAP` (F5).
5. **Keep the GL layout** — either write block-aligned (`13 + 13*b`) or change the
   workbook map/gate to the 12-row stride consistently; never clear rows beyond the
   written range (F6, F11).
6. **Reconcile the COA list with the sheet** — fix the duplicate `Paid-Up Capital` /
   missing `Retained Earnings (Deficit)` and refuse to run when the sheet's titles
   and the engine's account list differ (F7).
7. **Audit integrity** — append to `GL_AUDIT` with the 4-column
   `Timestamp | Status | Summary | Detail` layout instead of `Cells.Clear` (F8).
8. **Refresh `GL_V8_CALC` in the same run** (or retire it) so the two never
   disagree (F9).
9. **Scan or reject out-of-block CRJ/GJ data** so appended months cannot be lost
   silently (F10).
10. **Import the actual v8.2 modules** (`modGLRefresh`, `modGLAggregation`,
    `modGLGate`, `modGLWorkbookMap`) and remove/rename the legacy
    `RefreshGL`/`RefreshAllGL` in `modEngine` so there is one canonical entrypoint —
    the shipped workbook still has the legacy signatures (`RefreshAllGL(targetSheet)`
    ignores the year; `CalculateEndingBalanceFull` requires `startBalance`) (F12).

## 7. Limitations of this review

- The write path (probes B and C) was exercised on **scratch copies**; the reviewed
  workbook was opened read-only and its SHA-256 is unchanged
  (`CA7303CF…3EDB`).
- MsgBox dialogs were dismissed programmatically to let the macro finish unattended;
  the captured dialog texts are in the evidence files.
- Module-level compile state of the workbook's own VBA was not changed: no v8.2 BAS
  module was imported, so this review measures the **shipped** engine only.
